🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to HearHelper! Our app is designed to make communication accessible for everyone. Here's how to get started:

  • Transcription: Click "Get Started" to begin real-time speech-to-text conversion
  • Communication: Access enhanced communication tools from the menu
  • Emergency: Set up emergency contacts for quick access
  • Settings: Customize your experience in the settings page

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Alt + S Start/Stop listening
Alt + M Mute/Unmute text-to-speech

📋 Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the transcription?

Our AI-powered transcription achieves 95%+ accuracy in quiet environments with clear speech.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all communications are encrypted and we never store your personal conversations.

Can I use HearHelper offline?

Basic features work offline, but real-time transcription requires internet connection.
