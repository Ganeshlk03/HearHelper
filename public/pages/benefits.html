🇮🇳 Central Government Benefits

Disability Pension & Benefits

Central

Monthly pension provided by the Central Government for persons with disabilities under the Social Security Scheme.

Eligibility:

  • Age: 18 years and above
  • Disability: 40% or more
  • Below Poverty Line (BPL) status
  • Not receiving any other pension

Amount:

₹1,500 - ₹2,500 per month (varies by state)

Required Documents:

  • Aadhaar Card / Voter ID
  • Disability Certificate (40% or more)
  • Income Certificate
  • Bank Account Details

Railway Concession (50% Discount)

Central

Get 50% discount on railway tickets for persons with disabilities across India.

Eligibility:

  • Disability: 40% or more
  • Valid Disability Certificate
  • One attendant also gets 50% discount if accompanying

Coverage:

All classes: AC, Sleeper, Second Class on all trains

How to Avail:

  • Submit Disability Certificate at ticket counter
  • Book online on IRCTC with disability certificate
  • Show certificate at the time of journey

Income Tax Exemption

Central

Special tax deductions and exemptions for persons with hearing impairment.

Tax Benefits:

  • Standard deduction: ₹75,000 (increased from ₹50,000)
  • Higher deduction limit under Section 80U
  • Deduction for medical treatment expenses
  • Exemption on disability pension

Documents Required:

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Disability Certificate
  • Income proof

National Scholarship for Students with Disabilities

Central Student

Scholarship for students with disabilities studying at school, college, and university level.

Eligibility:

  • Disability: 40% or more
  • Annual family income: Below ₹2.5 lakhs
  • Merit in studies
  • Indian citizen

Amount:

  • School: ₹500-₹2,000 per month
  • College: ₹2,000-₹5,000 per month
  • University: ₹5,000-₹10,000 per month

Application Timeline:

Usually: June-July every year

Employment in Government Jobs (Reservation)

Central Employee

4% reservation in government jobs for persons with disabilities including hearing impaired.

Job Categories:

  • Central Government Jobs (UPSC, SSC, RRB)
  • Group A, B, and C positions
  • Posts suitable for hearing-impaired candidates

Special Concessions:

  • Scribe facility in exams
  • Extra time during exam (up to 1 hour)
  • Sign language interpreter if needed

📋 Important Information

How to Apply?

  1. Get Disability Certificate (40% or more) from authorized medical board
  2. Collect required documents (Aadhaar, income certificate, etc.)
  3. Visit relevant government office or apply online
  4. Submit application with complete documents
  5. Track application status online

Required Documents

  • ✓ Aadhaar Card / Voter ID
  • ✓ Disability Certificate (40% or more)
  • ✓ Income Certificate
  • ✓ Bank Account Details (for DBT)
  • ✓ Proof of Residence
  • ✓ Educational Certificates (if applicable)
  • ✓ Employment details (if applying for employee benefits)

Disability Certificate

  • 📍 Get from government hospital / medical board
  • 📞 Contact: District Hospital / Medical Department
  • ⏱️ Time: Usually takes 2-4 weeks
  • 💰 Cost: Usually free in government hospitals
  • 📋 Valid for: 5 years (renewable)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage of disability is required for benefits?

Most central government benefits require 40% or more disability. However, some state benefits may require 50% or more. Check specific scheme requirements.

How long is the Disability Certificate valid?

Disability Certificate is valid for 5 years. After that, it needs to be renewed by getting a fresh assessment from the medical board.

Can I receive multiple benefits at the same time?

Yes, you can avail multiple benefits simultaneously. For example, you can get pension, income tax exemption, and scholarship together, but some schemes may have income restrictions.

How is the disability pension transferred?

Disability pension is transferred directly to your bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). This ensures transparency and timely transfer.

What if my application is rejected?

You can appeal the decision within 30 days. Resubmit with correct documents or request reconsideration at the concerned department.

Are these benefits available for all states?

Central government benefits are available across India. State benefits vary by state. Contact your state's Social Welfare Department for state-specific schemes.
